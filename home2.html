Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our kits, engines, flight events, or getting started? Find quick answers below.

We recommend starting with our Skill Level 1 starter kits, such as the Alpha Scout or Starter Flight Pack. These feature snap-fit plastic fins, pre-colored components, complete launch controllers, and illustrated step-by-step guides ideal for builders ages 10 and up.

Yes, all our rockets are designed for repeated flights. After landing via parachute or streamer, you simply inspect the body tube and fins, install fresh recovery wadding, insert a new single-use rocket engine and igniter, and you're ready for your next liftoff.

Standard low-power rockets (using Class A through D motors) require no license or FAA waiver when launched in accordance with the NAR Model Rocket Safety Code. For high-power rockets (Class H and above), NAR or Tripoli certification is required, which our flight mentors can assist you with.

Altitude depends on the rocket design and motor class. Starter kits typically reach 300 to 800 feet, intermediate aerodynamic rockets achieve 1,000 to 2,000 feet, while our advanced composite and multi-stage models can soar beyond 3,500 feet into the sky.

Yes! Our organized community launch days welcome hobbyists of all experience levels. We supply electronic launch racks, launch pads, range safety officers, and flight guidance. You can bring any NAR-compliant rocket to fly on our dedicated flight ranges.
