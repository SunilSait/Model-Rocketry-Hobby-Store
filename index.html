How It Works

From Box to Launchpad in 4 Steps

Building and launching your first model rocket is easier than you think. Follow these simple steps.

1

Choose Your Kit

Browse our collection by skill level and find the perfect rocket kit for your experience.

2

Build & Customize

Follow the included instructions to assemble your rocket. Add paint and decals for a personal touch.

3

Load the Engine

Insert the appropriate motor, attach the igniter, and set up your launch pad in an open area.

4

Launch & Recover

Countdown, press the button, and watch your rocket soar! The parachute ensures a safe landing.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about model rocketry, our kits, and getting started.

Model rocketry is suitable for ages 10 and up with adult supervision. Our beginner kits are designed specifically for young builders, with simple snap-together construction and low-power engines that are safe and easy to use.

Low-power rockets (A-D class engines) do not require certification. However, for high-power rockets (E class and above), you'll need certification through organizations like NAR or TRA. We can help guide you through the certification process.

Model rockets should be launched in open fields away from buildings, trees, and power lines. Public parks (with permission), sports fields, and designated launch sites are ideal. Check local regulations and join our community events for organized, safe launches.

Absolutely! Most model rockets are designed for multiple flights. The recovery system (parachute or streamer) brings the rocket safely back to the ground. Simply replace the engine, repack the recovery system, and you're ready for another launch.

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. However, please note that rocket engines (motors) may have shipping restrictions to certain regions due to their classification. Contact us for specifics about your location.
